"Service that is constant, reliable, accurate, and transparent."

System for Information Profiling and Administrative Governance (SIPAG) is a localized information management web portal designed for Barangay Local Government Units (LGUs) in the Philippines that centralizes household and resident profiling data into an organized digital database to streamline local administration. The system accelerates service delivery, improves accuracy in household tracking, and simplifies resource distribution.
